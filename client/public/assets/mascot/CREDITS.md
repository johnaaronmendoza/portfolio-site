# Lil Johnny mascot: provenance

## Original frames (hand-made)

`lil-johnny-idle.png`, `lil-johnny-happy.png`, `lil-johnny-worry.png`, `lil-johnny-wrong.png`

Original pixel art by John Aaron Mendoza Branzuela, from his SCS project art. 39 x 64 px, transparent.
These files are the ground truth for the character's palette, outline, eyes and smile, and are not edited.

## Pose frames (`poses/`)

- `idle.png`, `happy.png`, `worry.png`, `wrong.png`: the four original frames above, unchanged,
  padded onto the shared 47 x 64 pose canvas (feet on the bottom row, centred on the feet).
- `lean-left.png`, `lean-right.png`, `crouch.png`, `jump.png`, `land.png`, `celebrate.png`,
  `thumbs-up.png`: AI-generated by John with ChatGPT from his reference art (one 4 x 2 pose
  sheet), then cleaned to the pixel grid in code: background removed, resampled to the native
  grid by per-block mode, colours snapped to the original 17-colour palette, outlines set to
  the original outline colours. The eyes on every pose frame and the smile on `celebrate` and
  `thumbs-up` were replaced with the hand-made eye and smile from the original frames, because
  the resampled 1 px details broke.
- `sheet.png` + `sheet.json`: all eleven frames above in one horizontal strip, with each frame's
  rectangle.

Build script (reproducible from the pose sheet):
`design-system/portfolio/asset-work/mascot-poses/build_poses.py`
