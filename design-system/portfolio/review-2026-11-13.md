- Plex Sans for reading text; no monospace paragraphs; CRT scanlines off by default.

### 2. Design decisions for John
8. **Bring the "AI tools people can trust" proof back to Home.** With the Desk Assist diagram removed, guardrails are only reachable by hovering the GUARD key or asking the chat (resume lines 28–36 have them). Expert's move: hero right column = `desk_assist.exe` six-step flow with "Agent reviews" filled yellow (yellow = AI site-wide), Lil Johnny small on its edge pointing at it ("I check every draft. Ask me how."). Lighter alternative: a 3–4 line "Built to be trusted" list under the LGA entry from resume wording only.
9. **One world, not four layers** (retro OS + comic + 3D plastic + sticker bomb). Keep the desktop as the world, comic as the narrator's voice; "Issue #NN" → "2025 · team of 6"; keep work-object stickers, drop off-topic ones (eyeball, anatomical heart, cookie, ace, coffee, arcade…); mascot only in hero + chat; yellow reserved for AI. Tone risk for a bank: bloody eyeball / anatomical heart beside Skills, "PLOT TWIST!" on the cancer-awareness tile.
10. **Stats band:** POW burst on coursework 89.61% is the loudest number; "4 SCS languages" is filler → lead with a work figure (after B2).
11. **3D keyboard on phones:** legends ~9px at 375 (house rule 12px) and a 245 kB gzip chunk → flat 2D board below 640px / coarse pointers, or less tilt.
12. Hero motion: ~10 simultaneous loops (particles, 9 pixels, 2 cursors, narrator bob/boil/wave/drift, scroll cue); particles tuned for a dark ground.
13. Halftone dots sit behind reading text (house rule) — back `Lead` / Skills intro with the page colour or keep dots in the gutters.
14. Fixed Pause/Sound dock covers the SilverLink tile title at 1366 and the "Scroll" cue at 375; 8 tab stops before "Download resume" (A13).
